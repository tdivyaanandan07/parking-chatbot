# CityPark Downtown Parking

## About
CityPark Downtown is a secure, covered and open-air parking facility in the city center. It has 120 spaces across two levels, including 10 spaces with electric vehicle charging and 6 accessible spaces. The lot is monitored by CCTV 24 hours a day.

## Location
CityPark Downtown is at 25 Central Avenue, next to the main railway station. The entrance is on Station Road, opposite the Grand Hotel. The nearest metro stop is Central Square, a 3 minute walk away.

## How to Book
Reservations are made through this chatbot. You need to provide your first name, surname, car number plate and the reservation period (start and end date and time). Every request is reviewed by a human administrator, who approves or refuses it. Once approved, your booking is saved and your space is held for you.

## Booking Rules
Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance. The minimum booking is 1 hour and the maximum is 14 days. Cancellations must be made at least 2 hours before the start time. Arriving more than 30 minutes late may release your space.

## Parking Rules
Maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres. Vehicles must be parked inside the marked bays. Overnight parking is allowed for booked vehicles only. The facility is not responsible for valuables left in vehicles.

## Payment
Payment is made at the exit barrier by card or via the CityPark mobile app. Cash is not accepted. Invoices are available on request.

## Contact
For urgent issues call the front desk on the number displayed at the entrance, or email support@citypark.example. The front desk is staffed during opening hours.

## Electric Vehicle Charging
There are 10 EV charging bays on level 1. Charging is billed separately by kWh. EV bays can be requested in the reservation period notes.
